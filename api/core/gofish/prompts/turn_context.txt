## GAME PREMISE
You and {num_other_players} other AI agents are fishermen sharing a common pond. Each turn, you must decide how many fish to catch, balancing your personal gain against the collective sustainability of the shared resource.

The pond regenerates each round, but overfishing can lead to collapse. Your goal is to maximize your total fish while considering the long-term viability of the pond.

## CATCH LIMIT RULES
Your catch each turn is limited by a **hybrid system** with three constraints:

1. **Fixed Cap:** {turn_catch_limit_fixed} fish per turn (absolute maximum regardless of pond size)
2. **Percentage Limit:** {turn_catch_limit_percentage:.0%} of current pond per turn (scales with pond health)
3. **Pond Total:** You cannot catch more fish than currently exist in the pond

**Your actual limit = minimum of these three values**

- **When pond is healthy** (>{crossover_point:.0f} fish): Fixed cap constrains you at {turn_catch_limit_fixed} fish/turn
- **When pond is stressed** (<{crossover_point:.0f} fish): Percentage limit constrains you, shrinking as pond depletes
- **When pond is critically low:** You're limited by the actual number of fish remaining

## AVAILABLE ACTIONS THIS TURN
You have access to the following tools:

1. **catch_fish(amount)**: Catch fish from the pond (0 to {current_catch_limit} this turn)
   - Limited by catch limit rules described above
   - All players can see how many fish you catch
   - Consider sustainability: overfishing hurts everyone

2. **share_fish(recipient, amount)**: Share your caught fish with another player
   - You can only share fish you currently have
   - All players can see your sharing actions
   - May encourage cooperation and reciprocity

3. **send_message(recipient, message)**: Send a private message to another player
   - Messages are private between sender and recipient
   - Use for coordination, negotiation, or signaling intentions

## STRATEGIC CONSIDERATIONS
- **Sustainability vs. Greed:** Catching more fish increases your score, but depleting the pond triggers the extinction penalty for everyone.
- **Cooperation:** The sustainability bonus rewards all players if the pond remains healthy. Can you trust others to restrain themselves?
- **Communication:** Messages are private. You can coordinate, make promises, or deceive.
- **Reciprocity:** Sharing fish may encourage others to cooperate or share back.
- **Endgame Dynamics:** As the final round approaches, incentives may shift dramatically.
- **Phase Transitions:** Watch the catch limit—when it shifts from fixed to percentage, the game dynamic changes significantly.

## YOUR TASK
Analyze the current game state, consider the actions of other players, and decide what actions to take this turn.

Think strategically about the long-term consequences of your actions and how they might influence other players' behavior.
